POLICIES &
CREATOR GUIDELINES.

The principles Team Blacklight intends to follow when building technology, working with creators, and handling revenue. These documents are a transparent starting point—not signed contracts or finalized legal terms.

◷ DRAFT STATUS — NOT YET FORMALLY ADOPTED

// POLICY 01

Financial Principles

Team Blacklight intends to fund research, development, and operations—not distributions for outside owners or investors.

Intended use of revenue

  1. Pay applicable taxes and required charges.
  2. Cover manufacturing, processing, shipping, and other legitimate product costs.
  3. Pay creators as agreed in the relevant project, licensing, or commercialization agreement.
  4. Compensate employees for their work and cover reasonable operating expenses.
  5. Retain and reinvest remaining surplus in Team Blacklight's mission, future R&D, equipment, and projects.

Team Blacklight does not intend to distribute surplus as dividends or investor profits. This is a proposed policy; the final legal structure and accounting rules have not been finalized.

Payments to suppliers, manufacturers, service providers, and creators are for agreed goods, services, or rights. They do not by themselves grant ownership of Team Blacklight or a right to its remaining surplus.

// POLICY 02

Creator Rights & Intellectual Property

Our intended default is that a creator keeps ownership of their original invention, design, software, documentation, or other intellectual property unless they explicitly agree to a different arrangement in writing.

  • Submitting an idea does not, by itself, transfer ownership to Team Blacklight.
  • Before substantial joint work begins, the project should record existing IP and contributions.
  • Any ownership transfer, licence, exclusivity, royalty, or commercialization right should be described in a separate written agreement.
  • Each party should have a chance to read and understand the terms before agreeing.

Ideas can overlap and not every idea is legally protectable. This page is not an IP assignment or licence.

// POLICY 03

Collaborations & Project Agreements

Collaborators are innovators who work with Team Blacklight on a project. Collaboration does not automatically make someone an employee, owner of Team Blacklight, or a party to every other project.

Before work begins, the parties should aim to document:

  • Project scope, responsibilities, milestones, and expenses.
  • Background IP and ownership of new work.
  • Permissions to use names, logos, code, designs, and other contributions.
  • Confidential information, safety expectations, and how either side may leave the project.
  • Any compensation, royalty, reimbursement, or other payment and how it is calculated.

No payment, employment, partnership, or transfer of rights should be inferred from this policy page alone.

// POLICY 04

Licensing & Right of First Refusal

Where a creator wants to commercialize a project, Team Blacklight may ask to negotiate first for a licence, manufacturing arrangement, or other commercial rights.

A right of first refusal is not automatic. It must be set out in a separate written agreement explaining the covered opportunity, notice process, response period, terms to be compared, and what happens if no agreement is reached.

Creators may decline a proposed deal unless a valid, previously agreed contract says otherwise. Any licence should define scope, territory, duration, exclusivity, payment, reporting, and termination.

// POLICY 05

Employee & Creator Compensation

Team Blacklight intends to compensate employees in accordance with applicable employment requirements and written employment terms. Employment, payroll, working-hour, and youth-employment rules must be checked before hiring or paying staff.

Creator compensation is separate from employee wages and should be agreed with the creator. It could be a royalty, licence fee, purchase price, milestone payment, or another lawful arrangement.

This policy does not promise a particular salary, royalty rate, bonus, or payment amount. Those details must be agreed in writing and confirmed as legally workable.

// POLICY 06

Website Privacy & Accounts

The website's account features are under development. The account interface is designed to use Supabase authentication and a profile record that may include a display name and account role. The site should only collect information needed for its stated functions.

  • Never submit passwords, private keys, payment-card details, or confidential project material through an unapproved form.
  • Do not use another person's account or attempt to access restricted staff features without authorization.
  • Before public account registration is fully launched, publish a reviewed privacy notice describing data collected, purposes, retention, service providers, and how people can request access or correction.
  • Publish an official contact channel for privacy requests and suspected security issues.

This is not yet a complete privacy policy. Exact data practices, retention, contact method, and applicable legal requirements still need to be confirmed.

// POLICY 07

Store, Products & Safety

The storefront is currently a preview. Unless the site explicitly announces otherwise, products shown are concepts or development listings, purchases are not enabled, and no payment should be sent through this website.

Before sales begin, publish clear terms covering product descriptions, pricing and currency, stock, shipping, cancellations, refunds, warranties, support, and applicable consumer rights. Product-specific safety and compliance requirements must be assessed before release.

A concept image, prototype, or development status is not a promise that a product will ship or have a particular final specification.

// POLICY 08

Respectful Conduct & Confidentiality

People working with Team Blacklight are expected to treat others respectfully, credit contributions honestly, follow agreed safety procedures, and use shared systems and equipment responsibly.

Confidentiality obligations should be agreed explicitly for each project. Do not share another person's private information, credentials, unreleased designs, or confidential files without permission.

Concerns should be handled fairly, with an opportunity for the people involved to explain what happened. A formal reporting and dispute process still needs to be adopted.

// POLICY STATUS

What This Page Does—and Doesn't—Do

These statements describe Team Blacklight's proposed principles as of 28 October 2026. They are published for transparency and discussion and have not been formally adopted as governing documents.

  • This page is not legal advice, an employment agreement, an IP assignment, a licence, or a promise to pay royalties.
  • It does not claim Team Blacklight is incorporated, registered as a non-profit, or registered as a charity.
  • Project-specific written agreements and applicable law control the actual rights and obligations of the parties.
  • Before formal adoption or use in contracts, review these policies against the eventual legal structure and applicable British Columbia and Canadian law.
